# TelePROmpter

A modern, minimalist, distraction-free teleprompter that runs directly in your web browser. It requires no installation, no sign-ups, and works completely offline on PC, Mac, tablets, and mobile phones.

---

## Key Features

- **Smooth Auto-Scrolling**: Effortlessly scroll through your speech or script at your preferred pace with adjustable speeds.
- **Eye-Tracking Reading Guide**: A signature blue guide line with pointer arrows helps you keep your place and look directly into the camera lens with confidence.
- **Customizable Countdown Timer**: Set a 1 to 10 second countdown before auto-scrolling begins, giving you time to look up, breathe, and get ready. You can tap the countdown or press Space to start immediately.
- **Dark Mode & Light Mode**: Switch between a sleek black background for studios or bright white background for daylight reading with one click or by pressing `T`.
- **Mirror Mode for Teleprompter Glass**: Instantly flip your text horizontally with a single button or by pressing `M` so it reads correctly when reflected on teleprompter glass rigs.
- **Vertical Inversion**: Invert text vertically if you have an upside-down monitor or mounted screen.
- **Distraction-Free Disappearing Controls**: Press `H` to make all controls vanish cleanly right in place so your screen is 100% focused on your script.
- **Script Editor with Auto-Save**: Paste or type your script anytime by clicking "Edit Script" (or pressing `Esc`). Your script, speed, font size, and preferences are automatically remembered for your next session.
- **Live Presentation Stats**: Keep track of your elapsed speaking time, estimated time remaining, and total word count in real time.
- **Adjustable Text Size & Margins**: Customize your font size and reading column width to fit any screen size comfortably.

## How to Open & Use

You don't need to install anything or host a server. You can run TelePROmpter simply by opening the file in your web browser.

### On PC / Mac / Laptop

1. Open your favorite web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox).
2. Click the browser's address bar at the top.
3. Type or paste the direct path to the file and press **Enter**:
   ```text
   C:\Users\John Jervys\Downloads\Teleprompter\TelePROmpter.html
   ```
   *(Alternatively, you can simply drag the `TelePROmpter.html` file and drop it into any open browser window).*
4. Press **F** to enter full screen, and you are ready to record or present!

---

### On Mobile Phones & Tablets (Android / iPhone / iPad)

You can use your phone or tablet as a portable teleprompter screen mounted on your camera tripod or desk.

#### Android:
1. Transfer or download `TelePROmpter.html` to your phone's **Downloads** folder.
2. Open Google Chrome.
3. In the address bar, type:
   ```text
   file:///sdcard/Download/TelePROmpter.html
   ```
   *(Or open your phone's **Files** / **My Files** app, navigate to Downloads, tap `TelePROmpter.html`, and choose to open with Chrome).*
4. In Chrome's menu (`⋮`), tap **"Add to Home screen"** to launch it like an app anytime!

#### iPhone / iPad (iOS):
1. Send or save `TelePROmpter.html` to your **Files** app (inside the "Downloads" or "On My iPhone/iPad" folder).
2. Tap the `TelePROmpter.html` file in the Files app to preview it, then tap the **Share** button and open with **Safari** or **Chrome**.
3. In Safari, tap the Share icon and select **"Add to Home Screen"** for a full-screen, distraction-free app experience.

## Quick Controls & Shortcuts

| Action | Shortcut | Button / Control |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Play / Pause** | `Spacebar` | Blue Play / Pause button |
| **Adjust Speed** | `Up Arrow` / `Down Arrow` | Speed slider (`5x`) |
| **Skip Back / Forward** | `Left Arrow` / `Right Arrow` | Drag screen or arrow keys |
| **Rewind to Beginning** | `R` or `Home` | Rewind icon button |
| **Hide / Show Controls** | `H` | Disappears controls in place |
| **Dark / Light Mode** | `T` | Sun / Moon icon button |
| **Mirror Text (Glass Rig)** | `M` | Mirror icon button |
| **Fullscreen** | `F` | Fullscreen icon button |
| **Edit Script** | `Esc` | "Edit Script" button |
